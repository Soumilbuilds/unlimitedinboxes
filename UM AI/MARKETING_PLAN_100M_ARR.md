Unlimited Inboxes provisions Microsoft 365 (Outlook) inboxes for cold email campaigns. Users connect via OAuth, upload a CSV, and launch campaigns through Smartlead/Instantly/etc.

**Current pricing:**
- Free: 100 inboxes
- Pro: $99/mo unlimited inboxes
- Agency: Custom

**Competitors charge $0.50-$9.00/inbox.** You charge $0.06. This is 8-150x cheaper. It's structural, not promotional — competitors literally cannot match this without losing money.

## 5. Acquisition Channels (Ranked by ROI)

### Channel 1: Cold Email Outbound (Irony Intended)
Yes, cold emailing cold emailers. It's the highest-ROI channel because:
- The product is for cold emailers
- Every campaign sent IS a product demo
- Cold emailers respect the channel and understand the math

**Approach:**
- Build list of cold email agency owners via LinkedIn Sales Navigator + Apollo
- Target founder/agency owner titles
- Message: Pure cost-savings ROI case study
- Send 200-500/day initially, scale to 2,000-10,000/day

### Channel 2: YouTube Creator Partnerships
Cold email YouTube is dominated by 5-15 influencers who drive tens of millions in buying decisions.

**Approach:**
- Identify 20-30 cold email YouTubers
- Offer 20-30% recurring revenue share for life of referred customers
- Offer $1,000-$5,000 per sponsored video (structured as honest review)
- Seed 3-4 in-house explainer/demo videos

**ROI Math:** One video with 100K views at 0.5% conversion = 500 signups = $49,500/mo recurring.

### Channel 3: Facebook Group Communities
"Cold Email Mastermind," "B2B Cold Outreach," "Email Marketing Mastermind" — 10K-100K+ members of exactly your ICP.

**Approach:**
- Deploy 2-3 team members as active community members (not spammers)
- Run 1 sponsored post/month in top 5 groups
- Answer questions, post case studies, offer free consultations
- Sponsor the group ($500-$1,000/month)

### Channel 4: Smartlead/Instantly Integration
Capture demand already searching for inbox integration.

**Approach:**
- Build deep integration documentation for Smartlead and Instantly
- Get listed in their app marketplaces
- Create YouTube tutorial: "Connect Unlimited Inboxes to Smartlead in 5 Minutes"
- Target SEO for "Azure inboxes for cold email"

### Channel 5: Paid Acquisition
Volume amplifier after organic validates, not a starting point.

**Google Ads keywords:**
- "cheap outlook inboxes for cold email"
- "affordable microsoft 365 inboxes"
- "[competitor] alternative"

**Budget:** $21K/mo (Phase 1) → $90K/mo (Phase 3)
